POLICY ID: POL-007
TITLE: Network Segmentation, Firewall Policies & Zero Trust Architecture
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: PCI-DSS v4.0 Requirement 1, ISO 27001 A.13.1, NIST SP 800-207

1. ZERO TRUST NETWORK ARCHITECTURE (ZTNA)
1.1. Network location (being on office Wi-Fi or corporate network) DOES NOT grant implicit trust.
1.2. All access to internal applications, microservices, and databases must be explicitly authenticated, authorized, and encrypted per session regardless of network origin.

2. NETWORK SEGMENTATION & FIREWALL CONFIGURATION
2.1. Production Environments must be isolated in dedicated VPCs/VLANs separate from Staging, Development, and Corporate networks.
2.2. Direct public internet inbound traffic is forbidden for internal database servers, backend microservices, and management ports (SSH 22, RDP 3389).
2.3. Bastion hosts or identity-aware proxies (Teleport / AWS SSM Session Manager) with session logging must be used for administrative remote access.
2.4. Firewall rules must be reviewed and audited every 6 months. Unused rules must be pruned immediately.
