POLICY ID: POL-015
TITLE: Security Awareness Training & Phishing Simulation Policy
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: ISO 27001 A.7.2.2, SOC 2 CC2.2, NIST SP 800-53 AT-2 / AT-3

1. MANDATORY TRAINING TIMELINES
1.1. Onboarding Security Training: All newly hired employees must complete mandatory interactive cybersecurity awareness modules within 14 days of employment start date.
1.2. Annual Refreshers: All existing staff must complete mandatory security compliance refresher training annually.
1.3. Role-Specific Training: Software engineers and DevOps staff must complete OWASP Top 10 Secure Coding training every 12 months.

2. PHISHING SIMULATIONS & PENALTY ESCALATION
2.1. Simulated Phishing Campaigns: SecOps conducts unannounced quarterly phishing simulation exercises.
2.2. Failure Escalation Framework:
   - 1st Failure (Clicking link/credential entry): Mandatory completion of 30-minute remedial phishing course within 5 days.
   - 2nd Failure within 12 months: Notification sent to direct manager and mandatory 1-on-1 SecOps coaching.
   - 3rd Failure within 12 months: Escalation to HR, suspension of remote work privileges, and formal disciplinary review.
