POLICY ID: POL-003
TITLE: Incident Response Plan & SLA Timelines
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: ISO 27001 A.16.1, NIST SP 800-61 Rev 2, GDPR Article 33

1. INCIDENT SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION & RESPONSE SLAs
The organization classifies cybersecurity incidents into four severity levels. Teams MUST adhere to the SLA timelines below:

1.1. Severity 1 (Critical - Active Data Breach / Ransomware / Production Down):
   - Acknowledgment SLA: < 15 minutes
   - Triage & Containment SLA: < 1 hour
   - Executive & Legal Notification SLA: < 2 hours
   - Regulatory Breach Notification SLA: < 72 hours (GDPR / HIPAA compliance requirement)

1.2. Severity 2 (High - Compromised Admin Account / Malware on Internal Workstation):
   - Acknowledgment SLA: < 30 minutes
   - Triage & Containment SLA: < 4 hours
   - Resolution SLA: < 24 hours

1.3. Severity 3 (Medium - Phishing Email Received / Suspicious Login Alert):
   - Acknowledgment SLA: < 2 hours
   - Investigation SLA: < 12 hours
   - Resolution SLA: < 48 hours

1.4. Severity 4 (Low - Non-critical policy violation / Minor vulnerability scan finding):
   - Acknowledgment SLA: < 24 hours
   - Resolution SLA: < 7 business days

2. POST-INCIDENT REQUIREMENTS
- Lessons Learned meeting must occur within 5 business days of incident closure for Sev 1 & Sev 2 incidents.
- Incident Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report must be completed and submitted to CISO within 10 business days.
