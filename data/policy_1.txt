POLICY ID: POL-001
TITLE: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) & User Authentication Policy
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: ISO 27001 A.9.4.2, NIST SP 800-63B, SOC 2 CC6.1

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy establishes mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) requirements across all enterprise systems, cloud platforms, VPN gateways, web applications, and administrator interfaces. Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing enterprise assets.

2. MANDATORY MFA REQUIREMENTS
2.1. All user accounts attempting remote access, cloud workspace login, email login, or infrastructure access MUST authenticate using at least two independent factors:
   - Factor 1: Something you know (Strong Password/Passphrase)
   - Factor 2: Something you have (FIDO2 Hardware Key, TOTP Authenticator App, or Push Notification)
2.2. SMS-based OTP and Email OTP are strictly PROHIBITED as secondary factors for internal personnel due to SIM-swapping and interception vulnerabilities.
2.3. Hardware tokens (e.g., YubiKey) are MANDATORY for all Privileged / Administrator Accounts (Domain Admins, AWS Root/IAM Admins, Database Administrators).

3. ENFORCEMENT & TIMEFRAME
- Re-authentication is required every 12 hours or upon IP/device change detection.
- Adaptive / Risk-Based MFA must trigger step-up authentication when login attempts originate from unknown locations or unrecognized devices.

4. EXEMPTIONS & EMERGENCY ACCESS (BREAK-GLASS)
- Break-glass administrator accounts must have credentials stored in a dual-custody hardware safe with automated alert notifications sent to the SOC upon use.
