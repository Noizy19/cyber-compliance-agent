POLICY ID: POL-005
TITLE: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Principle of Least Privilege
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: ISO 27001 A.9.1.2, SOC 2 CC6.1 & CC6.2, NIST SP 800-53 AC-2 / AC-6

1. PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE
1.1. All users and automated system processes must be granted ONLY the minimum necessary permissions required to fulfill their explicitly defined job duties.
1.2. Default Access Level: All new system accounts default to ZERO ACCESS (Deny All) until explicit role assignment occurs.

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) FRAMEWORK
2.1. Access is structured around pre-defined enterprise roles (e.g., Support Tier 1, Developer, QA Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Finance Admin).
2.2. Ad-hoc granting of direct permission entitlements to individual user accounts is strictly PROHIBITED; permissions must be tied to Okta / Active Directory security groups.
2.3. Segregation of Duties (SoD): Developers must NOT possess write/administrator privileges in Production environments. Production deployments must be automated via CI/CD pipelines with dual approval.

3. ACCESS REVIEWS & AUDITS
- Quarterly Access Reviews (QAR): System owners and team managers must review and certify user permissions every 90 days.
- Stale Access Removal: Accounts inactive for over 45 days are automatically disabled; accounts inactive for over 90 days are deleted.
