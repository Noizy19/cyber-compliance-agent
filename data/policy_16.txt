POLICY ID: POL-016
TITLE: Identity & Access Lifecycle (User Onboarding & Offboarding)
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-01-01
COMPLIANCE STANDARD: ISO 27001 A.9.2, SOC 2 CC6.2, NIST SP 800-53 IA-2 / IA-4

1. ONBOARDING ACCESS PROVISIONING
1.1. Access requests must be submitted via HR ticketing system with explicit manager sign-off.
1.2. Access must be assigned based strictly on pre-defined job role templates in IdP (Okta / Azure AD).
1.3. Temporary Access: Contractual or temporary access grants automatically expire after 90 days unless manually extended by sponsor.

2. OFFBOARDING ACCESS DEPROVISIONING SLAs
2.1. Involuntary Terminations (High-Risk Offboarding):
   - Access Deprovisioning SLA: IMMEDIATE (< 15 minutes post-notification).
   - IT SecOps disables SSO, revokes OAuth tokens, terminates active VPN sessions, and wipes corporate mobile container.
2.2. Voluntary Terminations (Standard Offboarding):
   - Access Deprovisioning SLA: Within 4 hours of employee departure or effective end of business day.
2.3. Equipment Return: Laptops, smart cards, and physical security keys must be returned within 3 business days.
